🎯 What Is This?

sitemapbuilder.co.uk is a powerful yet simple tool that takes your website's XML sitemap and transforms it into multiple visual formats. Whether you're a website owner, SEO professional, or just curious about your site's structure, this tool makes it incredibly easy to understand your website's architecture.

✨ Key Benefits:

• See your entire website structure at a glance

• Identify important pages and sections

• Spot potential SEO issues

• No technical knowledge required

📈 What Can You Create?

🌳 Tree View

See your website's hierarchy as an expandable tree structure. Perfect for understanding how pages are organized.

📊 Treemap

Visualize the size and importance of different sections. Bigger blocks mean more pages or content.

🌅 Sunburst Chart

Beautiful circular visualization showing the relationship between different parts of your site.

📋 Table View

Simple, sortable table format for easy scanning and analysis.

📊 Statistics

Get useful numbers like total pages, average depth, and more.

🚀 Getting Started - Windows Users

Step 1: Download the Application

Visit this link to download the application: https://github.com/Ryoowa1303/sitemapbuilder.co.uk/releases

Look for the latest release and click the download button. The file will be saved to your computer.

Step 2: Run the Application

Once downloaded, double-click the file to start it. No installation needed - it runs directly!

Step 3: Load Your Sitemap

You have two options:

  • Paste your sitemap URL (e.g., https://yourwebsite.com/sitemap.xml)
  • Upload your sitemap file directly from your computer

Step 4: Explore Your Results

That's it! Your sitemap will be instantly transformed into all the visual formats. Click between tabs to switch views and explore your website's structure.

🛠️ Technical Details (For the Curious)

How It Works

The application runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. It fetches your sitemap, parses the XML, and creates visualizations using D3.js. For sitemaps that block direct access, it uses a lightweight PHP proxy to fetch the data.

System Requirements

  • Any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
  • Internet connection (to fetch sitemaps)
  • No installation required - works directly in the browser
